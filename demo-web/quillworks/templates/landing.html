Boring where it counts.

Writing tools should get out of the way. The useful parts are the ones you only notice the day something goes wrong.

Version history that reads like a story

Every save is a named revision with an author and a timestamp. Restore a version in one click — the old text is never thrown away.

Tags that build the map

Tag a note once and Quillworks links it to everything else carrying that tag. The related-notes panel is the institutional memory.

Import without the mess

Paste an export link from your old wiki and we pull the text straight into a draft. Markdown and plain text work best; HTML gets cleaned up.

Workspaces, not folders

Each team gets its own space with its own members and roles. Owners handle billing, editors write, viewers read.

Comments in context

Discuss a paragraph where the paragraph lives. Comment threads stay attached to the note instead of drifting into chat.

Yours to leave

Everything exports as Markdown. No lock-in, no proprietary format, no support ticket required to get your own writing back.

The daily loop

Write. Tag. Find it again.

  1. Capture — a note is a title and a body. Nothing to configure.
  2. Connect — tags link notes across the workspace automatically.
  3. Review — history shows what changed, by whom, and when.
  4. Share — invite the people who need it with the role they need.
